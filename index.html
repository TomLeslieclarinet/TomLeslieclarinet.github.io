About me


“I love your sound! So romantic! 💕”
Sofia, Saxophonist from Santorini

Tom is a passionate and highly skilled clarinetist and saxophonist, specializing in crafting unforgettable musical experiences for luxury weddings and exclusive events. With a deep love for both music and people, he brings an extra layer of elegance, warmth, and personal touch to life’s most meaningful moments—ensuring that every celebration is as unique and memorable as the people it’s for.

As a husband, Tom understands the profound significance of love, connection, and shared experiences. He feels truly honored to be part of others’ most beautiful days, and he is deeply committed to making those moments even more magical through his music. Whether it’s the timeless elegance of a wedding ceremony or the lively celebration of a once-in-a-lifetime occasion, Tom’s music—whether on clarinet or saxophone—will enhance the emotions and memories of your day in ways that last a lifetime.

Born and raised in Hungary, Tom began his classical music education at the prestigious Bartók Béla Conservatory in Budapest, where he first fell in love with the beauty and depth of classical music. After completing his formal studies, he continued to refine his craft as an autodidact, exploring a diverse range of musical styles and performance techniques. While classical music remains a foundation of his artistry, Tom thrives on blending genres—from romantic melodies on clarinet to smooth, soulful tunes on saxophone—and working closely with clients to create musical experiences that reflect their vision and the unique atmosphere of their event.

Now based in Austria, Tom is available for events worldwide, offering bespoke musical experiences tailored to your style, taste, and story. Whether you’re planning an intimate ceremony, an elegant cocktail hour, or a vibrant celebration, Tom’s captivating sound, professional presence, and attention to detail will elevate your event and create an unforgettable atmosphere for you and your guests.

Explore Tom’s music and discover how his artistry—on both clarinet and saxophone—can add a luxurious, personalized touch to your next event. With Tom, every note tells a story, and every performance is designed to create lasting memories.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of events do you perform at?

I perform at weddings, proposals, anniversaries, corporate events, and other special occasions, adding a unique and elegant touch with live clarinet music.

What genres of music do you play?

My repertoire spans classical, jazz, pop, and more. I tailor my performance to match the atmosphere and style of your event.

Can I request a specific song that is not in your repertoire?

Absolutely! I’m happy to learn new pieces upon request. Please provide at least 4 weeks' notice to ensure a polished performance.

How long does a typical performance last?

Performance durations are flexible and can range from 15 minutes to 3 x 40 minutes, depending on your needs.

Do you bring your own equipment?

I bring my clarinet and necessary accessories. If amplification is required, please let me know in advance so we can arrange the best setup.

Where are you available to perform?

I primarily perform in Europe but am open to traveling. Additional travel fees may apply for distant locations.

How much does it cost to book you?

Pricing varies depending on the type of event, its duration, and location. Each booking is unique, so I provide a tailored quote after discussing your specific needs. Feel free to reach out for a personalized offer.

How can I book you, and what is the payment process?

You can book me by filling out the [Enquire] form on this website or contacting me via email or phone. Payments can be made via bank transfer or cash, with details provided upon booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

If you need to cancel, please notify me as soon as possible. Refunds or rescheduling will be handled based on the terms agreed upon in the booking contract.

What do you wear to performances?

I dress in elegant attire suited to the formality and style of your event to ensure a polished and cohesive appearance.

Can I extend the performance time during the event?

If my schedule allows, I’m happy to extend the performance. Please let me know in advance or during the event to discuss the details.

How far in advance should I book?

To ensure availability, I recommend booking as early as possible, especially for weddings and peak-season events. Last-minute bookings may be possible depending on my schedule.

Do you perform solo or with other musicians?

I primarily perform solo, but I collaborate with pianists, string quartets, and other musicians upon request. Let me know if you’re interested in an ensemble performance.

Can you help me choose the right music for my event?

Of course! If you’re unsure which pieces would best suit your event, I’d be happy to provide recommendations based on the atmosphere and style you envision.

Do you require a sound check before the performance?

For most events, a short sound check is necessary to ensure the best audio quality. I will coordinate the details with you or your audio team.

What happens if my event is outdoors?

I’m happy to perform at outdoor venues, but certain weather conditions (such as rain or extreme heat) may require additional arrangements to protect the instrument. Let’s discuss the details to ensure the best setup.

Do you provide a contract for bookings?

Yes, for clarity and peace of mind, all bookings include a written agreement outlining the key details, including date, time, location, and payment terms.
